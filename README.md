## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Repterra**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life controls for Repterra's colony-building and survival systems. It can assist with resource management, construction, unit deployment, dinosaur breeding and taming, hero progression, and battles against massive dinosaur hordes.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to build colonies, experiment with factions, breed and ride dinosaurs, test hero abilities, explore maps, or manage large-scale battles without the standard resource and timer restrictions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Repterra uses an RTS control scheme rather than a traditional character inventory or third-person movement system. Select human units, dinosaurs, heroes, or groups of units and issue movement commands across the map. Pause-and-command play supports careful planning during large engagements. The trainer does not replace normal selection or command controls; it assists by protecting units, restoring abilities, or removing supported production delays.

### Tools & Inventory

Repterra does not use a conventional player inventory. Progress is managed through colony resources, production queues, structures, faction technology, permanent unlocks, perks, units, dinosaurs, and hero upgrades. Resource and upgrade options affect the relevant colony systems directly, while instant production options apply to supported queues.

### World & Environment

Expand the colony by placing buildings, defenses, production lines, and power connections. Explore replayable maps, secure territory, and prepare for increasingly dangerous dinosaur attacks. Repterra includes multiple factions with different strategic emphases, persistent unlocks, customizable heroes, and a level editor with Steam Workshop support.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on defending the colony and reclaiming territory from enormous dinosaur hordes. Deploy human units, defensive structures, heroes, and tamed or bred dinosaurs. Human units can ride dinosaurs, and each rider-and-dinosaur combination can provide distinct special abilities. Use invulnerability, hero protection, cooldown, and ability options only when needed to preserve the intended strategy of positioning units, managing defenses, and responding to enemy waves.
